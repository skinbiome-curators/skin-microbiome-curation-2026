## A. ① 시험 전 설계 점검 + 낭비 계산 — 고객이 받는 1쪽

### A-1. 받은 계획 (07 의 실제 설계를 '계획'으로 둠)

| 항목 | 계획 |
|---|---|
| 대상 · 기간 | 한국 여성 25명(30–58세) · 같은 에센스를 4주 동안 하루 두 번 · 평소 쓰던 화장품은 그대로 ✅ |
| 측정 | 볼 면봉 · 0 · 2 · 4주 · 16S(V3–V4) ✅ · 피부 상태(수분 · 결 · 피지 · pH)도 같은 시점에 잼 ✅ |

### A-2. 결론

> **이 계획대로면 균 변화를 제품 효과로 말할 수 없습니다.**
> - 대조가 없고 시점마다 다른 런에서 읽으면, 제품 효과와 처리 차이를 가를 수 없습니다(판정 규칙 결정표 1번).
> - 아래 네 가지를 고치면 **'무처리 대조보다 더 변했나'를 가를 수 있는 시험**이 됩니다(B).
> - 필요한 시료 수는 흔들림에 달렸습니다. 07 수준이면 07 과 비슷한 78–102개, 더 크면 5–6배(최대 약 530개)입니다.
> - 이 설계로 받을 수 있는 최고 등급은 지금 **'조건부'** 입니다. 최고 등급('근거로 쓸 수 있음')의 기준은 아직 설계 중입니다.

  - **파일럿이 손해인 조건:** 흔들림이 15계절 수준이면 처음부터 58/군으로 고정한 설계와 인원이 거의 같은데, 기간만 두 배다 → '고정 58/군'도 선택지로 둔다.

### C-5. PIF 용 영문 근거 요약 1쪽 (견본)

> **Skin Microbiome Evidence Summary — sample (supporting document for the Product Information File)**
>
> **Product / study (anonymised).** Leave-on essence. Single-arm, before–after study with no control group: 25 Korean women aged 30–58 applied the product twice daily for 4 weeks while keeping their own skincare routines. Cheek swabs at weeks 0, 2 and 4; 16S rRNA gene amplicon sequencing (V3–V4); genus-level relative abundance. Skin hydration, texture, sebum and pH were also measured in the study but are not assessed in this summary.
>
> **Claims assessed.** (1) "Supports / balances the skin microbiome"; (2) "Increases *Cutibacterium* / *Staphylococcus*"; (3) a statement of the fact of testing.
>
> **Data quality (rule E0): confounding suspected.**
> - Each time point was sequenced on a different run (week 0: run 242; week 2: runs 241 and 244; week 4: run 243). No run contains two time points.
> - Six genera were detected at ≥1% in every week-0 sample and in ≤12% of week-2 and week-4 samples; together they made up a median 58.1% of week-0 reads (range 36.5–82.7%) and 0.6% at weeks 2 and 4.
> - No untreated control group. No negative (blank) controls reported.
>
> **Decision (draft rule set v2, 17 Oct 2026).** All three candidate genera: **"Not yet supportable"** (rule 1 — no control group, and time point is confounded with sequencing run).
>
> For reference only (not used for the grade): in our re-analysis, the week 0→4 change in *Cutibacterium* (log-ratio +1.46) depends on which genera are removed from the denominator. It shrinks to +0.15 (95% CI −0.09 to 0.40) when the six week-0-confined genera are removed, but stays at +1.22 to +1.42 under other detection thresholds.
>
> **Wording these data can support.** A statement of the fact of testing, without any benefit or microbial change, naming the population (e.g. "Tested on the skin microbiome of 25 Korean women aged 30–58 who used the product twice daily for 4 weeks.").
> **Wording these data cannot support.** Microbiome support, balance or modulation; "microbiome-friendly"; any genus- or species-level change.
>
> **Notes for the Responsible Person.**
> - "Presentations of a product's performance shall not go beyond the available supporting evidence" (Reg. (EU) 655/2013, Annex, 4(1)).
> - UK CAP guidance (Jan 2021): "Where feasible, they would expect human studies to reflect the population for which the product is intended." This study enrolled Korean women only.
> - Relative abundance only (no absolute quantification).
>
> **Next step.** A controlled design is attached as a design request: untreated control, baseline measured twice, swabs stored and processed (extraction, PCR, sequencing) together so that both groups and all time points share each batch, negative controls per batch, and a pre-specified analysis plan.
>
> **Reproducibility.** Input files fingerprinted (SHA-256); same data and rule set give the same result.
>
> **Scope.** This summary states whether these data support a given wording. It is not a safety assessment, not legal advice, and not a guarantee of efficacy. Claim wording and PIF sign-off remain with the brand and its Responsible Person.

- PIF 와의 관계: 규정은 PIF 에 *"where justified by the nature or the effect of the cosmetic product, proof of the effect claimed for the cosmetic product"* 를 두게 한다(1223/2009 제11조 ②(d) ✅). 이 요약은 그 근거 자료에 붙는 **판정 문서**이지 PIF 자체가 아니다.
